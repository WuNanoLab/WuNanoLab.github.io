We are pleased to announce the publication of our paper,["Core Size and PEG Coating Govern the Dual Magnetic Particle Imaging and Hyperthermia Performance of Iron Oxide Nanoparticles"](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsabm.6c01122), in ACS Applied Bio Materials. The article appears in the journal’s special issue, “Early Career Forum 2026.”

Magnetic particle imaging (MPI) and magnetic hyperthermia therapy (MHT) both depend on the dynamic magnetic response of magnetic nanoparticles, but the particle properties required for strong imaging contrast and efficient heating do not always align. This study systematically examines how iron oxide core size and PEG surface coating influence dual MPI-MHT performance.
