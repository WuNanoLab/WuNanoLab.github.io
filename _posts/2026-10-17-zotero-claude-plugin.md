The Wu Lab has released **Zotero Reading Bridge**, an open-source Zotero plugin that lets Claude read the papers already in your library. Ask a question about the PDF you have open, compare every paper in a collection, or have Claude summarize the highlights and notes you have already made, with answers grounded in the actual text rather than a web summary. The plugin is read-only and your library stays on your own computer.

**Try it:** [github.com/volatile-static/zotero-reading-bridge](https://github.com/volatile-static/zotero-reading-bridge)


We built it because literature review is one of the slowest parts of graduate research, and because it is also where new students learn the most. With the bridge, Claude becomes a reading partner that already knows what you have collected.

**The lab now runs on Claude.** The Wu Lab has been verified for Anthropic's [Claude Team plan for scientists](https://claude.com/programs/team-plan-for-scientists). Every lab member gets a seat at no cost, with access to the Opus models, [Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code) for analysis and simulation scripts, [Claude Science](https://claude.com/product/claude-science) for data analysis and database search, and connectors to PubMed, bioRxiv, and ClinicalTrials.gov. Conversations and research data under the plan are not used for training.

Every student admitted to USF and joining the lab automatically qualifies for a seat; no application is needed. Researchers who would like to try the plugin or collaborate on AI-assisted literature tools are welcome to reach out.
