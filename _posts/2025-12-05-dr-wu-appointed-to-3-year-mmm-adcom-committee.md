Dr. Wu has been appointed as the AIPP-representative Voting Member of the [MMM Advisory Committee (AdCom)](https://magnetism.org/2026/mmm-adcom) for a three-year term ending in December 2028. In this role, he will help advance the mission of MMM by supporting and strengthening the magnetism and magnetic materials research community.

  

The AdCom meets virtually on a monthly basis and is responsible for voting on the locations and dates of future MMM and joint meetings, selecting future conference chairs and officers, and providing strategic guidance on other key society initiatives.
