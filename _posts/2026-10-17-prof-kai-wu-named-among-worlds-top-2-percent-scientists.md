We are pleased to share that Prof. Kai Wu has been named among the World's Top 2% Scientists in the latest edition of the list, recognized in the Single-Year Impact category under the Applied Physics subject field.

The World's Top 2% Scientists list is released annually by Stanford University in collaboration with Elsevier, based on standardized citation indicators from the Scopus database. The Single-Year Impact category recognizes researchers whose work has made a significant impact over the most recent year.
