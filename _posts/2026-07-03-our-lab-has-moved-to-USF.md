We are excited to announce that our lab has moved to the University of South Florida in Tampa, Florida. Dr. Kai Wu has joined the Department of Physics as a tenure-track Assistant Professor.
At USF, our lab will continue to advance interdisciplinary research at the interface of magnetic materials, magnetic devices, and biomedical applications. We look forward to building new collaborations and expanding our research efforts in magnetic nanomaterials, spintronic devices, biosensing, magnetic particle imaging, and bioelectromagnetic technologies.
