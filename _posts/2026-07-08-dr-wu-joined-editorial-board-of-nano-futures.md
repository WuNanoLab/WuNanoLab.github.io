Dr. Kai Wu has joined the Editorial Board of ["Nano Futures"](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/journals/nano-futures/editorial-board/), an IOPscience journal that publishes research at the forefront of nanoscience and nanotechnology. This appointment follows the conclusion of his editorial board member role with Nanotechnology, another IOPscience journal.
In this new role, Dr. Wu will contribute to the journal’s mission of advancing high-quality research in emerging areas of nanoscale materials, devices, and applications.
